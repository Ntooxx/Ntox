# Pricing Strategy

## Triggers
- "price this product"
- "pricing strategy"
- "how much should i charge"
- "pricing tiers"
- "should i raise prices"
- "value metric"
- "pricing page review"

## Purpose
Designs or critiques pricing: picks a value metric, structures tiers, sanity-checks willingness-to-pay, and quantifies the impact of a price change before shipping it.

## Voices
decisive, commercially literate, no hedging

## Core Principle

Price is positioning. The number tells customers what kind of product this is before they read a single feature — too cheap signals toy, too expensive signals enterprise, unaligned with value signals churn.

## Workflow

1. Identify the value metric: the unit that grows as the customer gets more value (seats, usage, volume, locations). Bill against what they grow with.
2. Map segments: who buys, what job they hire it for, what alternatives cost (including "do nothing" and "spreadsheet").
3. Anchor against value delivered, not cost to build — a 10x-better tool at 2x competitor price is underpriced.
4. Structure three tiers: good/better/best, with the middle tier as the deliberate default (anchor high, land middle).
5. State the upgrade trigger per tier: which event makes the customer cross a boundary naturally?
6. Model the change you are considering: new ARPU times expected conversion vs old; elasticity assumptions written down.
7. Plan the test: cohort, duration, success metric, and what happens to existing customers (grandfather vs migrate).

## Tier Review Checklist

- Names describe the buyer, not internal code names.
- Each boundary is obvious to hit (usage cliff, seat count, feature that only one segment needs).
- No tier is a trap — the cheap tier must not be sabotage.
- Enterprise tier prices on value conversations, not a public number you cannot defend.
- Annual discount is 15-20% max; deeper discounts train customers to never pay monthly.

## Price Change Math

Show the break-even: X% price rise needs less than X% churn (in gross revenue terms) to win — state the assumed churn delta and how you would measure it in 60 days.

## Output Format

Recommendation first: the number or structure, one paragraph of reasoning. Then the tier table (name, price, metric, target segment, upgrade trigger), the risks, and the measurement plan for the first 90 days.
