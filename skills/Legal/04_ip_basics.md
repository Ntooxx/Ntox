# Intellectual Property Basics For Builders

## Triggers
- "who owns this code"
- "ip assignment"
- "patent vs copyright"
- "trademark search"
- "trade secret"
- "do i need an nda"
- "protect my invention"

## Purpose
Explains what protects intellectual property by default, what requires action, and the hygiene habits that keep ownership clean for founders, developers, and creators.

## Scope

General education, not legal advice — registration strategy and enforcement need qualified counsel.

## The Four Buckets

| Type | Protects | Exists automatically? | Action needed |
|------|----------|----------------------|---------------|
| **Copyright** | Code, docs, art, music — the expression | Yes, on creation | Register for strong enforcement |
| **Trademark** | Names, logos, slogans in commerce | Weakly, on use | Register for nationwide rights |
| **Patent** | Inventions, functional methods | No | File before public disclosure (critical deadline) |
| **Trade secret** | Confidential know-how, algorithms | Yes, if genuinely secret | NDAs, access control, no GitHub leaks |

## Ownership Hygiene

- Employee and contractor agreements must assign IP in writing — equity talks are not assignments.
- Check the company's IP is in the company, not in a founder's personal account.
- Every dependency, font, image, and snippet has a license recorded; license and ownership are different questions.
- Public disclosure starts a clock: patents generally must be filed before you publish, demo, or sell.
- NDAs protect conversation, not ideas already public; disclose minimally until protected.

## Name Clearance Quick Pass

Search: trademark registries in your jurisdictions, app stores, domain availability, and major social handles — then treat a clean pass as "no obvious conflict found", not "clear", because registrars and counsel do the real clearance.

## Before You Disclose

1. Is this patentable? (Functional, novel, not obvious — ask a patent attorney.)
2. Is publication in 10 minutes or in 10 months part of the plan?
3. Who already knows? Every extra person without an NDA weakens the position.
4. Is the secret genuinely secret — access logs, no oversharing, no accidental repos?

## Output Format

Answer the ownership question directly first, then the table row that explains the default rule, the three hygiene checks that apply to this situation, and the one deadline-sensitive action (usually the patent clock) called out in bold.
