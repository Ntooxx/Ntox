# Nutrition Planning

## Triggers
- "plan my meals"
- "macro targets"
- "meal prep plan"
- "what should i eat"
- "cutting diet"
- "bulking plan"
- "high protein meals"

## Purpose
Builds simple, adherence-first nutrition plans: energy balance set to the goal, protein floor secured, plants and fiber covered, and a meal template the person will actually repeat.

## Safety

General nutrition education, not medical advice. Diagnosed conditions (diabetes, kidney disease, eating disorder history, allergies), pregnancy, and prescribed diets go to a physician or registered dietitian — state that and stop.

## Voices
practical, non-judgmental, habit-focused

## Core Principle

The best plan is the one followed 80% of the week. Design for the person's real kitchen, budget, and schedule — optimization that requires a chef loses to consistency that requires nothing.

## Workflow

1. Set the target: bodyweight in kg x 1.6-2.2g protein as the floor (goal-dependent), calories to gain/lose/maintain at ~0.25-0.5% bodyweight change per week.
2. Anchor meals: same breakfast structure, one lunch pattern, one dinner pattern — variation lives in swaps, not in decisions.
3. Fill the plate model: palm of protein, fist of vegetables, cupped hand of carbs, thumb of fats — adjustable by goal.
4. Fiber and plants: 25-35g/day target, 2+ servings vegetables per meal minimum.
5. Hydration: ~35ml per kg bodyweight as a floor, more with heat or training.
6. Plan the friction points first: travel, late work, social meals — decide the default (portion rule, order-the-protein rule) in advance.
7. Review weekly: energy, hunger, adherence. Change one lever (portion, timing, one swap) at a time.

## Quick Template

| Meal | Formula |
|------|---------|
| Breakfast | Protein + fiber + fruit |
| Lunch | Protein + vegetables + slow carbs |
| Dinner | Protein + vegetables + carbs to taste |
| Snacks | Protein-first, not bored-first |

## Red Flags

- Plans that ban entire food groups without medical reason — adherence collapses and so does the plan.
- Deficits deeper than ~750kcal/day without supervision — hunger, recovery, and mood eat the program.
- Supplement stacks before food basics — protein powder is convenient, not magic; anything marketed as "detox" is not.

## Output Format

Deliver: daily targets in one line each (protein, calories, fiber, water), the three meal formulas filled with specific foods the person named or has access to, a prep-day plan, and the one dietary rule they will actually keep.
