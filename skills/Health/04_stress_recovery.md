# Stress And Recovery Management

## Triggers
- "i'm overwhelmed"
- "stress management"
- "burnout risk"
- "too much on my plate"
- "recover from overwork"
- "mental load"
- "feel exhausted"

## Purpose
Audits a person's stress load, separates controllable from structural sources, and installs recovery practices that fit their week — treating recovery as scheduled work, not leftover time.

## Safety

Not mental-health treatment. Hopelessness, panic, sustained inability to function, or thoughts of self-harm go to a professional or crisis line immediately — lead with that when signs are present.

## Voices
calm, empathetic but action-oriented, no platitudes

## Core Principle

Stress is not the enemy — unrecovered stress is. Load and recovery are two dials: when the load dial cannot move (deadline, season), the recovery dial must be raised deliberately or the system fails.

## Workflow

1. List every active source of stress with a one-line cost: work, money, health, people, uncertainty.
2. Mark each: controllable (act), influenceable (prepare), structural (accept and protect around).
3. Rate current load 1-10 and recovery 1-10 — the gap is the risk number.
4. Pick the controllables with the biggest cost: defer, delegate, renegotiate scope, or drop — three items maximum, not thirty.
5. Raise recovery on four levers and schedule them like meetings:
   - Sleep consistency (the master lever)
   - Movement: 20-30 minutes of easy activity most days
   - Human contact that is not transactional
   - True downtime: absorbing, screen-light, non-productive
6. Install one shutdown ritual: end-of-day list for tomorrow, close the loops, physically leave the desk.
7. Set the tripwires: two weeks of the same warning signs (sleep wrecked, dreading everything, no pleasure) means re-run the audit or seek help.

## Load Signals

| Early | Late (act now) |
|-------|----------------|
| Irritable, shallow sleep, procrastination | Dread, exhaustion at rest, cynicism, getting sick often |

## Output Format

Deliver: the source table with verdicts, the three reductions chosen and what replaces the freed capacity, the weekly recovery schedule as actual calendar blocks, and the personal tripwire they agreed to watch.
